Ads and Remove Ads

Ads appear only between matches, never during a battle, and not after every match. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase on the main menu and the post-match screen. After reinstalling, or on another device with the same Apple Account, open Remove Ads and tap Restore purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you can change your ad consent choice from "Ad privacy choices" in the main menu.

Saves and learning

Your battle saves itself: reopen the app to carry on where you left off. The tutorial is on the main menu: you can go back, skip ahead or pick a step at any time.

Sound, speed and graphics

Sound and music can be switched on or off from the menu or the pause screen. Speed up a battle with the 1×–4× button; in 2 Players both players tap their own speed button to agree. If the game feels slow, set Graphics to Fast.

Two players

Choose 2 Players to play a friend on the same device. The layout button next to pause changes the table layout.
